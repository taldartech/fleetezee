The Industry Is Changing

Transportation moves the world. Its technology should move with it.

Millions of journeys, vehicles, documents, payments and decisions power transportation every day. Yet much of the industry still operates across spreadsheets, paper, WhatsApp and disconnected systems.

THE FRAGMENTED PAST STATUS QUO

Disconnected Silos

Critical freight knowledge trapped in notebooks, chats, and fragmented memory.

  • ✕
    Paper LR & Bilty: Physical slips lost in transit, delayed proof of delivery, manual re-entry errors.
  • ✕
    Excel Chaos: Unsynchronized trip sheets across branches, broken formulas, hidden freight leakages.
  • ✕
    WhatsApp Groups: Vehicle locations, diesel slips, and driver advances scattered without audit trails.
  • ✕
    Manual Billing & TDS: Invoices generated weeks late, forgotten GST reverse-charge, untracked deductions.
  • ✕
    Zero Real-time Visibility: Transporters discover trip profitability only 45 days after journey completion.
