Company Story

Technology for the businesses that keep the world moving.

Fleetezee was founded on an unapologetic belief: transportation is the backbone of physical civilization, yet it has been systematically underserved by modern software. We are changing that permanently.

Who We Are

A Transportation Technology Company

We are not a freight broker, not a trucking fleet operator, and not an ERP reseller. We are pure software engineers, systems thinkers, and product designers dedicated to the transportation industry.

Our flagship product, TruckBill, provides the immediate operating foundation: digital LR generation, GST freight billing, Section 194C TDS, driver trip settlements, and live voyage profitability.

Around TruckBill, Fleetezee is engineering the broader infrastructure: automated optical document ingestion, predictive corridor analytics, open transport APIs, and automated telematics networks.

How We Operate

Our Guiding Principles

The non-negotiable engineering and operational standards behind every Fleetezee product.

01

Absolute Ledger Integrity

In transport accounting, a single missing invoice or incorrect TDS percentage compounds into legal disputes. We engineer financial precision down to the exact paisa.

02

Uncompromising Simplicity

A branch manager in a remote hub should be able to create a legally compliant LR in under 30 seconds without attending weeks of training seminars.

03

Proactive Intelligence

Software must do more than record the past. It must actively protect margins, flag credit default risks, and alert operators to expiring road permits.

04

Long-Term Infrastructure

We are building software for decades, not quick exits. We treat transport technology as vital civil infrastructure that must never go down.
