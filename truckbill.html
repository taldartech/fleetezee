Product Architecture

Five Integrated Engines. Zero Disconnect.

Everything in transportation is interdependent. When a trip is dispatched, it informs the billing ledger, the fuel balance, the tax liability, and the profit margin.

01 — OPERATE

Trips, LR & Multi-Branch Dispatches

Manage dispatch cycles from consignor loading to consignee delivery. Generate professional GST-compliant Lorry Receipts in seconds with automated WhatsApp copies to shippers.

DISPATCH WORKFLOW STATUS: ACTIVE
Loading Slip → LR Generation → E-Way Bill NIC Verification → Driver Trip Advance → POD Tracking
  • • Multi-branch management with individualized serial numbers
  • • Driver license validation and performance tracking
  • • Both owned fleet & market hire vehicles handled in unified ledger
02 — BILL

Freight Invoices & Section 194C TDS

Stop waiting weeks to bill clients. Combine multiple LRs into single corporate freight invoices. Handle detention charges, loading labor, and automatic TDS deductions with total accuracy.

BILLING AUTOMATION RECONCILIATION: 100%
Freight Charges + Halting/Detention - TDS (1% or 2%) - Advance Adjustments = Net Due
  • • Automated Section 194C TDS calculation with Form 26AS matching
  • • Custom invoice templates for diverse consignor requirements
  • • Real-time aging report for outstanding customer receivables
03 — CONTROL

Expenses, Settlements & Vahan Compliance

Reconcile diesel card balances, toll slips, and driver trip advances before settling the voyage. Receive proactive automated alerts for fitness, national permits, and insurance renewals.

DISBURSEMENT CONTROLS ZERO LEAKAGE
Pump Card Integration + FASTag Auto-Pulls + Driver Batta Limits = Settled Balance
  • • Diesel slip entry with mileage consumption monitoring (km/L)
  • • Automated VAHAN compliance tracking for vehicle fitness & taxes
  • • Complete tyre lifecycle management and recap logs
04 — UNDERSTAND

Voyage-Level P&L & Lane Intelligence

Know the exact rupee profitability of every trip before the truck departs for its next load. Isolate high-margin lanes, identify loss-making routes, and optimize fleet deployment.

FINANCIAL INSIGHT REAL TIME P&L
Gross Freight Revenue - Total Voyage Direct Costs = Net Trip Margin
  • • Vehicle-wise profitability statements across monthly periods
  • • Customer margin analytics to support contract negotiations
  • • Real-time ledger accounting with Chart of Accounts
